Wiffletree for iPhone · screen mockups

Static mockups for approval before any UI is built. Each page shows its screens in an iPhone frame (393 × 852 points). The switch at the top right shows Light, Dark, or Both side by side, on every page. Colours are the Tidal tokens; type is the iOS system font.

Navigation: a tab bar per host (Projects, Inbox, Usage, Settings) with push navigation inside each tab, sheets for pairing and adding hosts, swipe actions on rows. Design notes and open questions are in design/README.md.
